# Discovery: Research & data landscape (Stage 1)

### Stage summary
Stage 1 now establishes the MedCore discovery baseline around five connected ideas:
- the problem is not only fragmented patient records, but fragmented **roles, workflows, channels, and governance** across formal and semi-formal care settings
- MedCore needs a **multichannel strategy** across `QR`, `USSD`, `Android`, and `Web`
- the underlying system should use a **FHIR R4-aligned canonical model** rather than different schemas per channel
- role-specific workflows matter for Patients, Doctors, Nurses, Lab Technicians, Pharmacists, and Health Records Officers
- privacy, audit, provenance, consent, and disclosure control must be part of the core architecture from the start

### Deliverables
- **Research brief (problem space overview)**
  - reframes the discovery question around multichannel, role-aware, offline-first EHR design in Nigeria and similar Global South contexts
- **Competitive landscape**
  - positions MedCore against hospital-centric EMRs such as **Helium Health**, open-source LMIC platforms such as **OpenMRS**, Android/mobile-companion categories such as **mUzima**, disease-program systems, and paper-based alternatives
- **Interoperability notes (FHIR resources + integration approach)**
  - defines FHIR R4 as the baseline and clarifies how channels map to one shared model
- **Compliance & privacy checklist (initial)**
  - captures role- and channel-specific privacy, security, disclosure, and audit needs
- **Data inventory + data requirements**
  - expands the inventory from field lists to role/domain/workflow-aware data requirements
- **Insights summary (top findings + implications)**
  - synthesizes the strongest findings shaping MVP and architecture direction
